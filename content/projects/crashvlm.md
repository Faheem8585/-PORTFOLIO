## The Question

Can open vision-language models read a crash test video, or is most of what they appear to see just the clock burned into the frame?

## Data

Public NHTSA NCAP database. 1,583 tests narrowed to 100 frontal, side-barrier and side-pole tests, two high-speed camera views each, giving 200 clips at 8 ms frames. Vehicle-grouped split so the same car never appears on both sides.

## What the Repository Contains

- Open data pipeline with overlay removal, an automated leak check and OCR time recovery
- A frame-level event ontology and 200 annotated views with documented label-quality checks
- A leakage-aware evaluation protocol
- Baselines running from a blind no-video predictor, through SigLIP 2 linear probes, to Qwen VLMs writing structured JSON crash reports
- Physics ground truth taken from vehicle accelerometers, and a clock-free optical-flow method validated against it
- A FastAPI service, a Gradio demo and a five-page paper draft

## Findings

**The clock gives the answer away.** With the lab's impact time visible, a predictor that never looks at the video at all scores 1.00 on airbag deployment and 0.94 on head contact. Any timing benchmark built on standardised crash tests is meaningless without this baseline.

**Even without the clock, the prior is strong.** A blind baseline times impact and airbag within 16 ms in 75% of cases. No frozen-feature probe or zero-shot encoder beat it.

**Video VLMs win where precision matters.** Within an 8 ms tolerance, all three Qwen models time airbag deployment significantly better than the prior, with Qwen3-VL-8B reaching 0.90 against 0.25. The same model also claims fire in 7% of clips where there is none.

**Fine-tuning helps even when cut short.** A LoRA run that completed 20 of 183 planned steps on a free GPU still improved impact timing significantly at 8 ms.

## A Result That Overturned Itself

An earlier version of the blind baseline was measured on uncropped clips, which made it around 20 ms late and therefore too weak. Fixing that measurement overturned an earlier conclusion that probes beat the prior. That correction is documented in the repository rather than quietly removed.

## Engineering

78 unit tests in CI with ruff. Hydra configuration. A frozen split file. Every number in the README is generated by `scripts/paper_tables.py`: nothing is typed in by hand.
